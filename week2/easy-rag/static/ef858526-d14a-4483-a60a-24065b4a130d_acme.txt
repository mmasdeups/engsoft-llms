Acme Robotics was founded in 2019 in Barcelona by Dr. Elena Vidal.
The company builds warehouse robots. Its flagship product is the Carrier-7,
a mobile robot that can lift up to 45 kilograms and runs for 9 hours on one charge.
Acme Robotics has 120 employees and its CEO is Dr. Elena Vidal.
The Carrier-7 was released in March 2023 and costs 18,000 euros.
